Community Events

Team 1912 is committed to inspiring the next generation of STEM leaders through community engagement, educational initiatives, and promoting robotics and technology throughout our community.

Women in STEM

Each year, we invite young women from our community to participate in our Women in STEM event, where they can explore many opportunities available in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. Women working in professional STEM fields related to that year's FIRST theme lead interactive stations that showcase their careers and experiences. Through hands-on activities, demonstrations, and presentations, participants gain firsthand insight into different STEM professions and are encouraged to see themselves as future scientists, engineers, innovators, and leaders.

FIRST Saturdays

Once every month, Team 1912 Combustion invites kids from our community to our build space to engage in a STEM-related activity with our team members.

FLL Qualifier

Once a year, Team 1912 Combustion hosts the FIRST LEGO League (FLL) qualifier event, a local, one-day official tournament where student robotics teams compete. High-performing and top-awarded teams at these qualifiers earn advancement bids to regional or state championship tournaments.

FTC Qualifier

Team Combustion hosts a FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) qualifier which brings together student-led robotics teams to compete in a head-to-head competition along with alliance challenges. Spectators are welcome and encouraged to watch and cheer for these students.
